# September 4 Bring-Up

## Objective
Bring up STM32 hardware and switch an external low-voltage load through an N-channel MOSFET.

## Parts Used

### MOSFET
Data sheet: https://www.onsemi.com/pdf/datasheet/nds7002a-d.pdf 

DigiKey number: 2N7000FS-ND

Manufacturer: onsemi

Manufacturer part number: 2N7000

Package: TO-92

Pin 1: Source

Pin 2: Gate

Pin 3: Drain

Maximum VDS: 60 V

Maximum drain current (continuous): 200 mA at 25°C ambient

Gate-source threshold voltage, VGS(th):

- Test conditions: TC = 25°C, VDS = VGS, ID = 1 mA

- Minimum: 0.8 V

- Typical: 2.1 V

- Maximum: 3.0 V

## Calculations

### Measurement Calculations

The LED current was calculated using the measured voltage across the
series resistor and its measured resistance:

$$
I_{\mathrm{LED}}
= \frac{V_R}{R}
= \frac{3.01\ \mathrm{V}}{321\ \Omega}
= 9.38\ \mathrm{mA}
$$

The LED forward voltage was calculated from the measured supply, resistor, and MOSFET voltages:

$$
V_F = V_S - V_R - V_{DS(\mathrm{on})}
$$

$$
V_F = 4.99\ \mathrm{V} - 3.01\ \mathrm{V} - 0.04\ \mathrm{V}
    = 1.94\ \mathrm{V}
$$

The measured LED current and forward voltage are both within the expected ranges.

## Problems

### ST-LINK USB Connection

A direct USB-C connection between the Nucleo board and the MacBook was unreliable. The board was detected reliably when connected through a USB hub. The USB hub was therefore used for programming and debugging.

### Resistance Measurement

The multimeter initially displayed an over-range reading while measuring
the resistors. The cause was poor contact between the probes and the
resistor leads. After improving the probe contact, the nominal 330 Ω
series resistor measured 321 Ω, which is within its expected tolerance.

The final measured resistor values were:

- Nominal 220 Ω gate resistor: 213 Ω
- Nominal 330 Ω LED series resistor: 321 Ω
- Nominal 10 kΩ pull-down resistor: 9.75 kΩ


## Conclusion

STM32 pin PB10 successfully controlled the red LED through the 2N7000
low-side MOSFET switch. The measured LED current was 9.38 mA and the
calculated LED forward voltage was 1.94 V. Both values were within their
expected ranges. The drain voltage changed from 3.34 V while OFF to
0.04 V while ON, confirming that the MOSFET switched the load.

When the GPIO connection was removed, the 10 kΩ pull-down resistor held
the MOSFET gate at 0.00 V and the LED remained OFF. This confirmed that
the circuit defaults to a safe OFF state when the controller output is
disconnected or inactive.
